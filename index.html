I am a Ph.D. student at Arizona State University and currently a visiting researcher at Harvard University. My research focuses on bridging theory and practice in machine learning, with an emphasis on responsible AI. I leverage tools from information theory, coding theory, optimization, and statistics to design algorithms that are both theoretically grounded and practically relevant. Recently, my work has explored privacy-preserving learning with differential privacy and model authentication through watermarking in large language models.
